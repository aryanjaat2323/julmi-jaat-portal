# 👑 JULMI JAAT — The Official Imperial Portal & Swag Hub

A high-octane, royal-themed imperial web application designed specifically for **Julmi Jaat**.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. ⚡ Amazing Cinematic Entrance
- **Speech Synthesis Voice Greeting**: The browser speaks out aloud: *"Hii Julmi Jaat, what can I help you with today? Hukum kijiye!"*
- **Typewriter Text Reveal**: Dynamic typing animation on a glowing royal card.
- **Cinematic Sub-bass Boom**: Deep synthesized entrance sound effect.
- **Floating Gold & Crimson Embers**: Interactive HTML5 particle canvas background.

### 2. 🎵 Desi Bass & Royal Beat Player ("जाटां का छोरा")
- **Official Song Integrated**: Loaded with **जाटां का छोरा (Jaatan Ka Chhora)**!
- **Real-Time Reactive Audio Spectrum**: Frequency visualizer canvas bounces and pulses in sync with the song's live beats and bass.
- **Playback Controls**:
  - Spinning Vinyl disc animation with glowing royal border.
  - Interactive Scrubbing / Seek bar with time display (`0:00 / 3:41`).
  - Rewind 10s (`⏪ 10s`) & Forward 10s (`10s ⏩`) quick skip buttons.
  - Volume slider.

### 3. 📸 High-Definition Photo Vault
- **All 7 User Photos Included**: Pre-loaded directly into the gallery showcasing **Julmi Jaat** and squad!
- **Instant Drag & Drop / File Picker**: Drop more photos anytime; saved locally with IndexedDB persistence.
- **Filter Tabs**:
  - *All Photos*
  - *Squad & Yaar 🦁*
  - *Uploaded by You ⭐*
  - *Royal Rides 🚜*
  - *Swag & Crest 👑*
- **Fullscreen Lightbox**: Click any photo to zoom and inspect in high definition.

### 4. 🦁 Julmi AI Command Assistant
- **Microphone Voice Recognition**: Speak or type commands.
- Asking *"Play song"*, *"Play music"*, or *"गाना बजाओ"* automatically starts **जाटां का छोरा** and scrolls to the sound system!

### 5. 🚜 The Royal Garage
- **Mahindra Thar 4x4**: Interactive turbo rev button with throat rumble audio.
- **Royal Enfield Bullet 350**: Iconic heavy flywheel dug-dug exhaust thump.
- **John Deere 5310 & Scorpio Classic**: Zameendar muscle cards with sound effects.

### 6. 📜 Attitude Shayari & Status Maker
- Curated collection of royal Jaat attitude quotes.
- 1-Click WhatsApp sharing and 1-Click Instagram Story Card generator (1080x1920 PNG download).

### 7. 🎨 Royal Theme Switcher
- Toggle between **Liquid Gold**, **Crimson Flame**, and **Cyber Matrix** themes.

---
